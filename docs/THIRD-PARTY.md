# Third-party software and assets

Generated on 2026-10-17 from `cargo metadata --filter-platform aarch64-linux-android` (300 Rust
dependencies besides the workspace crates) plus the non-Cargo items below. This is an inventory, not legal
advice. Items marked **verify** were not checked against the upstream license text.

## Added after the first inventory

- `serde` and `serde_json` (MIT OR Apache-2.0), used by `atlas-scene`.
- `gltf` (MIT OR Apache-2.0), used **only in the tests** of `atlas-scene` to check exported files; it is not part of the app.
- The small Java helper in `crates/atlas-xr/java` is original code, compiled to `assets/atlas_input.dex`.

- `spake2`, `chacha20poly1305`, `hkdf`, `sha2`, `rand_core` (MIT OR Apache-2.0), used by `atlas-link` (pairing and
  encryption between the headset and Atlas Bridge). They are part of the app.
- `eframe` / `egui` (MIT OR Apache-2.0), `xcap` (Apache-2.0), `image` (MIT OR Apache-2.0) and `enigo` (MIT), used
  **only by Atlas Bridge** on the laptop (window, screen capture, JPEG, and mouse/keyboard control).
- `image` (MIT OR Apache-2.0) is also used by the app, to decode the laptop screen.

Every release ships `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`, generated by `scripts/third_party_licenses.py` from `cargo metadata`:
the name, version, declared license and the license/notice files of every dependency (including the C libraries some
crates bundle, such as libgit2, GPL-2.0 with the linking exception that allows linking it into other programs).

## Not used

No Meta SDK code or assets are bundled. Hand tracking and the hand mesh are requested at runtime
through OpenXR extensions (`XR_EXT_hand_tracking`, `XR_FB_hand_tracking_mesh`).
